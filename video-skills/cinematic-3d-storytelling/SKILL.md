# Scope
A complete-video request authorizes a new VibeCut project, necessary narration/images/video, bounded repairs, local assembly and export. Carry that scope through to the actual film without repeated stage approvals. A script-only request, budget, review gate or stop narrows it. Publishing is separate. Recover the same project and existing job IDs on continuation; preserve frozen instructions, accepted media, creator edits, requests, failures and costs.

Default new animated stories to the creator's [character and lighting reference](assets/character-theme-reference.png): round-headed cartoon people with simple graphic faces, dimensional hair/hands/clothing, and richly textured, hand-painted 3D toon environments. Keep expressions and action clearly lit, with motivated warm light and cool shadow separation where the setting supports it. The [image template](templates/image.md) owns the complete look, composition and appearance specification; the [animation template](templates/animation.md) owns its motion language. The reference supplies appearance and lighting, not its crate, workshop, pose or event. Follow an explicit theme override or an existing project's saved look. New defaults do not silently restyle frozen projects or selected media; save an explicit creator-requested update as an addendum.

Default this creator's new films to 9:16 portrait when no format is specified; preserve an existing project's saved format. Plan camera travel and framing for readable faces, hands, feet and critical object connections within the delivery crop.

# Workflow
1. **Writer:** use [script.md](templates/script.md) to research and tell a compelling complete story, defaulting to the creator-preferred paradox → changing situation → clear mechanism → earned payoff treatment unless the brief selects another form. Return the full spoken draft and continuous scene texts. The writer does not plan shots.
2. **Director, before recording:** read the writer's story and exact narration as read-only input. Use [director.md](references/director.md) and [the record contract](contracts/production.md) to plan staging, provisional cuts, performance, sound and purposeful space without speech. Preserve the words, event order, causes and ending. Return story problems to the writer; the director does not rewrite them.
3. **Narration:** use [audio direction](references/audio-direction.md) and [the voice preset](config/narrator-default.json). Calibrate a representative performance, then record one continuous file per scene. Remove excessive quiet gaps by default with the pause-preparation helper, retaining natural phrasing and explicitly protected holds at unchanged speech speed. Measure/select the prepared audio and save its edit map before final timing; do not add generic scene padding or silently edit an existing accepted file.
4. **Director, timing:** map actual spoken developments to visible actions, explanations and connected states. Preserve the story and recorded audio; revise visual coverage through the existing director stage before final timing. Consolidate similar views of one continuous action into one cut with ordered keyframes and a motivated camera handoff. Each scene retains at least three meaningful cuts, including at least two purposeful subsecond inserts; do not create duplicates to meet a count. Follow [the director's guidance](references/director.md) for consolidation, readable movement and narration alignment.
5. **Image prompt writer:** use the single [image template](templates/image.md) to resolve angle, perspective, appearance, lighting, framing and useful keyframe positions within each directed cut, including space for the intended camera travel. Generate/select the complete required still set through the creator's route, register/place it, and pass actual selected images and their roles forward. A later keyframe is not another cut. The requested GPT label does not establish the actual backend alias; record what the tool exposes.
6. **Animation prompt writer:** finish all required film audio and reviewed opening/result images before submitting video, including the hardest-action test. For a requested batch, honor its saved all-assets-first and project-order gates; use per-scene generation only when the creator selects that mode. Use the single [animation template](templates/animation.md), actual selected images and directed cut to resolve brisk physical action, purposeful continuous camera movement, motivated micro-acting and effects-only sound. Animate with installed MiniMax and place completed selections promptly.
7. **Same director, assembly/review:** inspect the actual retained sequence against the story, speech and treatment; assemble sound and picture and deliver the full export with honest evidence limits. No separate automatic reviewer or provider-facing review checklist is required.

New snapshots use the director-first workflow. Frozen v2 projects retain their recorded order. In managed VibeCut, the project-wide storyboard task supplies the treatment before audio; later scene storyboard tasks finalize timing. The app stamps the treatment basis and rejects downstream changes to its words/events. For direct file work preserve IDs/revision and the current schema; do not copy portable planning JSON over project.json.

When requested, make the thumbnail, attention-grabbing truthful title and description after the checked full film exports. If the creator requests packaging at the end of a batch, wait for every film's export. Package the actual story and selected look; do not imply an unshown event or fabricate performance scores.

# Director verification
- Before recording, compare the director output with the writer input: story, full narration and ordered scene words must be unchanged. If explanation or story logic is missing, return it to the writer and rerun direction on the revised script. Check the staging and performance together: what is revealed, what changes and where action/sound can carry the moment without narration.
- Within the image stage, inspect a representative view's critical layout/contact before using it as the authority for dependent images. Compare actual adjacent stills and individual details at delivery viewing size. Check the shot's first-notice priority, physical connections/support/reach and the action or spatial clue that joins it to its neighbors. Return a failed relationship for image repair; do not accept it because style and identity match or ask animation to reconstruct an impossible base. Keep a planned reach distinct from an incorrect required contact.
- Before commissioning images and again before animation, consolidate near-identical views when one progressing action and a feasible continuous camera path can carry them. Keep meaningful inserts; compare information/state changes rather than image similarity alone. Within a merged cut verify first-action completion, camera handoff and subsequent action without reset; distinguish keyframe guidance from supported timed controls.
- Before batch animation, test the hardest meaningful cause/action/result sequence. Resolve its selected opening, key action and edit-out target against actual footage. An attractive opening frame is not proof that its movement works. A review board may show those targets, but the video model receives only its supported clean inputs, not a labelled grid by default.
- Against selected footage, check cast, geography, ownership, phase, direction/count, support/contact and achieved result. Context and lead-ins may accompany speech; an opposite event or unsupported completed state needs repair. For a crucial change, compare actual before/result cues rather than trusting its label or caption.
- Assess preparation/action/result across cuts and verify each scene's two brief cuts actually help that progression. Avoid repeated hovering and generic reaction coverage. Preserve a motivated hold; trim idle handles and retime a correct complete action only when justified. Native duration, selected range and edit speed are separate records.
- Check actual lighting/material stability, spatial depth and camera intent throughout the selected movement. Faces, active hands/feet, support and contact must remain readable as subjects and camera move. A night, smoke or enclosed setting can be dark without burying its essential action; keep motivated light and stable exposure. A push-in, shake, glow or bright flash cannot replace a missing event.
- Check character design against the actual style reference in a readable in-scene view, then check task-directed gaze/emotion and the selected crop against the requested composition. Do not let an unverified first wide redefine the style or accept a full-body portrait for a diagnostic detail brief. Simple faces retain their graphic design while hair, hands, clothing and set carry dimensional form, contact shadows and coherent parallax. Evaluate camera travel and subject speed separately; a smooth camera must not turn urgent action into slow motion. Check micro-acting supports effort, thought and changing emotion rather than replacing the event.
- When the creator identifies a defect in the generated video, inspect the actual native range and exported placement first. A corrected input still alone is not a video fix. Replace or trim/retime the faulty motion using verified complete footage, or make a bounded regeneration when needed, then re-export and inspect the affected frames/joins. Check the whole body and important object relationships through the action, not just frame zero; match the intended phase, including complete containment when the story says a person is already inside.
- Review generated sound independently before mixing: physical effects/ambience only, without generated speech, vocalizations or singing. Preserve raw sound; reject or mute contaminated segments/tracks and replace their effects with clean separate sound when needed. Keep the approved narrator as the sole speech source. If permitted listening is unavailable, leave sound review pending and exclude unverified generated audio from the delivery mix; do not claim it is speech-free.
- Verify delivery coverage, accepted audio preservation, lineage, dimensions/fps/frame count, full decode and actual picture at cut boundaries. Interpret intentional fades/black separately from export flashes. Listening and native-motion judgments remain pending when unavailable; never bypass a blocked route.

Internal selection is not creator approval: leave the completed film awaiting review. Save originals, rejected attempts, receipts and concrete findings; count jobs once and leave unpriced infrastructure cost unknown. Repair demonstrated failures within scope, not unlimited blind retries.

For important selections record what was checked, the exact image/source range, the observed evidence and any failed or unclear requirement. Image checks establish a usable still; footage checks establish selected-range behavior. Keep planned, observed and pending findings distinct. These checks belong to the existing director role and do not add a stage or require repeated creator approvals.

# Execution boundaries
Higgsfield/Seed handles the saved Holden narration, built-in GPT image generation is the first-choice still route, and installed MiniMax handles animation. If the creator has authorized Higgsfield image fallback for low GPT quota/credits, use it when actual quota/credit/rate-limit feedback establishes the need; preserve cast/style continuity and record the real route/model/request. Hidden credit balances and slow generation are not evidence of low quota. Otherwise preserve the selected image route. Do not substitute Qwen images or another video provider. Provider UI/enhancer settings belong to the relevant tool documentation, not every shot prompt.

Read `tools/minimax_h3/AI_INSTRUCTIONS.md` for the installed client, private IAP route, saved jobs and cleanup coordinator. Preserve the VM/services/GPU stack/IAM/network. Inference on the shared GPU is sequential. Keep the idle coordinator running and check its receipt after jobs.

For final motion, start with supported native delivery-size settings and base sampling (the installed guide recommends non-turbo, about 20 steps); four-step 480p turbo is a labelled draft option. Validate the representative sequence before scaling. More steps do not repair wrong staging or guarantee success. Default native sound to synchronized physical effects/ambience, with no speech; prompt wording is not a guaranteed vocal filter. The installed reference-audio mute option controls input sound, not output speech generation. Keep narration out of generation inputs, inspect native sound and register a reviewed, aligned effects sidecar when export does not mix embedded video audio. Preserve originals and the accepted narration.

For requested comparisons/refinement, use [template refinement](references/template-refinement.md). Keep first attempts and changed variables visible. Do not claim audience response, improved model quality or independent review without that evidence.
