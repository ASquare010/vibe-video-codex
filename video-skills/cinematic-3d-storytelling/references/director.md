# Director: treatment, timing and the viewing experience

The director owns the film's developing experience, spoken treatment, events, cuts, shared world and final verification. Image writing resolves concrete composition/angle/appearance; animation writing resolves physical action and camera travel. Give them a clear creative job without writing their technical prompts here.

## Before narration: direct the whole story
Read the full researched draft, ending, factual limits, brief and available references. Decide what the audience should anticipate, discover and feel as the situation changes. Rewrite unrecorded narration where its order, phrasing or emphasis can work better with the unfolding scene. Preserve the researched meaning and classification; return unsupported claims to research. A new compelling telling is useful, a disconnected replacement plot is not.

Plan visible developments alongside the final words. Narration need not name every movement. Establishment, approaches, urgent reactions, a loaded pause or the consequence of an action can carry a moment while speech leads, follows or briefly stops. For factual reconstruction, distinguish supported events from dramatized behavior in the production notes; do not invent evidence or private dialogue.

Choose provisional cuts by the information and experience they add. A scale-setting view can build anticipation, a short detail can supply a decisive cause, and a reaction/reveal can show what it changes. Make the actual event develop; repeated people inspecting something is not automatically a sequence. For an explanation, develop a relationship, not merely a succession of related objects.

For every important beat establish:
- The objective/question, pressure or obstacle and the new information.
- Incoming phase and shared geography, intended action/discovery, outgoing state and what it enables next.
- A visible before/change/result cue for a load-bearing development. Keep inferred, elided and continuously demonstrated completion distinct.
- The spoken passage, delivery intention, meaningful non-spoken space and sound intention.

Return the full spoken treatment plus scenes/cuts using [the record contract](../contracts/production.md). In managed VibeCut return complete ordered scenes with their existing IDs, plan.story/fullNarration, direction, spokenStyle and at least three provisional cuts per scene. Each cut has a purpose, exact narrationAnchor and startState/endState; its frame values remain provisional. Camera prompts may stay empty. Current recorded speech and its delivery are locked unless their revision is authorized.

## After narration: finalize timing
Use the selected audio version/path/hash, measured duration, available alignment and any pause-edit map. Finalize cut intervals against that file, preserving treated words, cut identity/order, purpose and event states. In managed VibeCut the app stamps the project treatment basis; scene timing results add directionBasis and change frame intervals, not the treatment. If an actual script/event change becomes necessary, return it to treatment and deliberately revise dependent audio rather than hiding it in visual prompts.

At least three meaningful cuts belong to each scene; total and uneven durations follow the event. A brief operation may take under a second or several seconds. Do not make every verb a cut, assign equal lengths or stretch a quick motion over its provider's native handle.

Evaluate preparation, movement and result across the whole sequence. Carry a reach into contact instead of restarting it in each view. Reserve time for the actual operation and an exposed result after the preceding setup. Suspenseful holds earn their duration through expectation; incidental empty waits do not. A timed board/animatic is provisional if playback is unavailable.

## One world, useful handoffs
Keep stable cast/prop/set IDs, scale, positions/facing, routes, supports, ownership, condition, knowledge and light. Match the meaningful event phase, not only its subject name. World locations remain fixed across angles; screen directions are camera-relative.

Give each prompt writer the same compact packet:
- Previous actual outgoing frame/state, or explicitly planned state if not generated.
- Current cut/beat ID, speech window, new information, evidence cue, opening phase and required outgoing state.
- Next required incoming state and join type: continuation, reaction, new location or deliberate ellipsis.
- Selected duration and intended action phases; actual references, source range/rate and evidence status when available.

Neighboring events inform the writers, but only the current take belongs in the provider prompt. Missing coverage returns to the director; a wrong opening image returns to the image stage. An abstract mechanism may need a supported explanatory view/graphic rather than invented hardware behavior.

## Selection and assembly
Compare actual adjacent stills without their descriptive titles. Keep repeated composition when changed content earns it; otherwise redirect/consolidate coverage or ask the image role to expose the missing relation. A new lens number alone is not a new idea. Validate critical geometry with the available blockout tools when needed.

Compare actual before/result cues at delivery viewing size. Inspect the complete useful movement before choosing source in/out; trim idle handles, then consider justified retiming while preserving contact, support, momentum and result. Keep native output, source selection and edit duration separate. Align effects with observed final contact, and mix ambience/physical sound beneath narration with intentional quiet where useful. A caption, click or flash cannot certify an obscured operation.

Distinguish narration mentions from measured sound effects. Do not invent word/impact timestamps or listening findings. Return for the actual-output and export checks in SKILL.md; preserve partial or unreviewed findings honestly.
