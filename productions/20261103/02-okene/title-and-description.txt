TITLE
60 Hours Inside a Sunken Ship | Harrison Okene

DESCRIPTION
Divers entered the wreck expecting to recover the dead. Then a hand reached back.

In 2013, Harrison Okene survived roughly 60 hours in a trapped air pocket inside the overturned Jascon 4, about 100 feet below the ocean’s surface. This animated retelling follows his refuge, the diver who found him, and the controlled recovery needed to bring him out safely. Eleven crewmates died.

AI-assisted animated reconstruction; not original rescue footage.
