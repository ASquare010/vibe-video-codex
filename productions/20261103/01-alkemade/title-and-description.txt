TITLE
He Fell 18,000 Feet Without a Parachute—and Survived

DESCRIPTION
His parachute was burning. Staying inside the bomber meant facing the flames. Nicholas Alkemade jumped into the night without it.

This animated history short follows the RAF gunner’s extraordinary 1944 survival, the pine branches and snow that helped break his fall, and the evidence that convinced his captors his account was true. He survived with injuries—not unharmed.

AI-assisted animated reconstruction based on historical accounts; these are illustrations, not archival footage.
