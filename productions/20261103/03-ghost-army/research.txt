Unit official history reproduced by Ghost Army Legacy Project: https://ghostarmy.org/thearchive/official-history-of-the-23rd-operation-viersen/ — March18–24,1945, simulation of30th and79th Divisions while actual divisions assembled in XVI Corps zone; coordinated larger Ninth Army deception; all media employed; captured German overlay placed79th near its represented position. Intelligence credited material assistance in deception. This is evidence of mislocation, not proof every German observer was fooled or that decoys alone caused crossing success.
No exact lives-saved number, no invented quotes/private thoughts. Cast identities, individual gestures, field layout and colors are illustrative reconstruction. Inflation is a compressed depiction of a process, not a claim of historical real-time speed. Tanks do not drive under their own power; men carry/position lightweight decoys. Aerial perspective depicts intended perceptual mechanism, not a documented particular reconnaissance flight. Radio is Morse-style activity without invented historical spoken messages. Sound operation shows period playback/amplifier and vehicle-mounted loudspeakers, not modern digital hardware. Captured map is a clearly simplified explanatory reconstruction, not a fabricated facsimile archival document.
