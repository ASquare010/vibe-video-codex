The Fake Army That Fooled Germany | WWII’s Ghost Army

DESCRIPTION
The tanks were inflatable. The soldiers carrying them were real.

Meet the Ghost Army: the U.S. unit that combined dummy equipment, recorded military sounds and false radio traffic to mislead German forces during World War II. This animated short shows how those clues reinforced one another—and how Operation Viersen helped create a false picture of American positions before the Rhine crossing.
