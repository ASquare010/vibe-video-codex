A rail mechanic aboard a moving freight train discovers that the rear wagon is burning. A tunnel is approaching. The mechanic must separate the burning wagon before the train carries the fire inside. Build a clear, escalating situation with a physical complication and an earned ending.

## Evaluation after production

This is an original fictional action premise, not a factual railway reconstruction or a technical instructional film. The creative challenge is physical readability and tension, not the number of explosions or a predetermined camera pattern.

Evaluate whether the audience can follow the route/problem, the attempted solution, obstacle, essential contact/change and final consequence; whether the voice has distinct pressure and release; and whether motion has weight, coherent depth and natural cadence. Check narration versus actual selected actions and joins. Preserve failed takes and distinguish a successful rewrite/repair from first-pass output. Native playback/listening and creator feedback are required to judge those experiences; frame counts alone are insufficient.
